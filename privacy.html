# Rot-Pet Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 7, 2026 Rot-Pet ("the app") is a screen-time app for Android. A virtual brain gets healthier when you use your phone better, and the app asks you to pause before opening apps you chose to limit. **In short:** Rot-Pet works fully offline. It has no account, no server, no ads and no third-party tracking. Everything it knows about you stays on your phone. ## 1. Data we collect **We do not collect any personal data.** The app has no Internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your phone to us or anyone else. All data the app uses is created and kept **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage. ## 2. Data stored on your device To work, Rot-Pet keeps this information locally on your phone: | Data | Why | |---|---| | The apps you pick to limit, your daily time budget, your brain's name | Your settings | | Minutes spent per app per day (from Android's Usage Access) | Rot Score, stats, weekly report and "before vs now" proof | | Check-in events (when a check-in appeared, whether you left or unlocked, challenge results) | Game progress: rot level, brain cells, streak | | A list of installed apps (name and icon) | So you can pick which apps to limit | | In-app activity counts (for example "report shared", "blocking turned off") | The optional beta stats summary (see section 5) | This data never leaves your phone unless **you** share something yourself (see section 5). ## 3. Permissions and how they are used ### Accessibility Service ("Rot-Pet App Check-ins") Rot-Pet uses Android's AccessibilityService API **only to detect which app is opened**, so it can show a check-in screen when you open an app you chose to limit. - It listens only for "window changed" events, to read the package name of the app in front. - It is configured so it **cannot read screen content**. It does not read, collect or store your messages, passwords, what you type, or anything shown inside other apps. - It does not change, click or control anything in other apps. - Nothing it observes is sent anywhere. You turn this service on yourself in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time. The app clearly explains this before asking. ### Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) Used to read how many minutes you spent in each app, so the app can show your stats, Rot Score and weekly report. The data is processed and stored on your phone only. ### Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Used for the optional daily 9 PM verdict and for alerts when blocking stops working (for example if the accessibility service was turned off). You can turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings. ### Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Used to restore your daily reminder and the blocking health check after the phone restarts. ### List of installed apps Rot-Pet asks Android for apps that appear in your launcher, so you can choose which ones to limit. This list is shown on your phone and not sent anywhere. ## 4. Third parties Rot-Pet does **not** use advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or any other third-party service that receives your data. It does not sell or share data with anyone. ## 5. What you can share yourself Some features let you share content **by your own choice**, using Android's standard share sheet (for example to WhatsApp or email): - **Weekly report card:** an image of your weekly results. - **Beta stats:** a short text summary with totals only (for example "check-ins: 12, walked away: 8"). It contains **no app names** and no personal details. You can read the full text before sharing it. - **Feedback:** the message you write, with beta stats attached if you choose. If you open the feedback form link, it opens in your browser. Anything you submit there is handled by that form's provider under its own privacy policy. Once you share something, the app you shared it with handles it under its own privacy policy. ## 6. Data retention and deletion - Data stays on your phone for as long as the app is installed. Old activity records are trimmed automatically. - To delete everything, **uninstall Rot-Pet** or clear its data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Rot-Pet → Storage → Clear data). - Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. ## 7. Children Rot-Pet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. ## 8. Security Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under Android's standard security model. Since nothing is sent over the Internet, it is never exposed in transit. ## 9. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending data off your phone, this policy will be updated first and the app will tell you clearly before it happens. ## 10.
